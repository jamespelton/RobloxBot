## Report: Crystal Siege Roadmap to $500/month Revenue

### Executive Summary
Crystal Siege should aim for **$500/month by first building retention, then monetizing durable identity and convenience rather than raw power**: the project already has a wave-survival loop, classes, building, loot, gems, lobby, weather, admin tools, and DataStore persistence, so the fastest path is polishing onboarding, repeat goals, store UX, and creator-friendly distribution before adding more combat scope ([local codebase brief](file:///Users/jamespelton/Apps/React/RobloxBot/CLAUDE.md)). At Roblox's current post-September-5-2025 DevEx rate of **$0.0038 per earned Robux**, $500/month requires about **131,579 earned Robux/month**, and if experience purchases net approximately **70%** after platform fee, the experience needs about **187,970 gross Robux/month** in player purchases before DevEx conversion ([Roblox DevEx support](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/13061189551124-Developer-Exchange-Help-and-Information-Page), [Roblox developer-products docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)). The recommended roadmap is a 12-week sequence: Weeks 1-3 pre-launch polish, Weeks 4-6 soft launch and monetization instrumentation, Weeks 7-9 growth loops and content updates, Weeks 10-12 conversion optimization and recurring revenue tests ([Roblox analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics), [Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)).

### Options Explored

#### Option 1: Retention-first polish, then monetization
- **Description**: Improve first-session clarity, round pacing, combat feel, lobby goals, social/co-op incentives, and store instrumentation before scaling acquisition; this fits Roblox's own analytics model because acquisition, engagement, retention, and monetization are tracked as separate dashboard areas that should be optimized together rather than treated as one launch metric ([Roblox analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics), [Roblox retention docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)).
- **Pros**: Better Day 1 and Day 7 retention should improve the amount of monetizable traffic available to the shop, because Roblox documents retention as the percentage of users returning after first play and provides Day 1, Day 7, and Day 30 views for diagnosing whether players come back ([Roblox retention docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)). Better onboarding and engagement also support discovery because Roblox's discovery documentation says recommendations use signals including user interactions and engagement when ranking experiences ([Roblox discovery docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)).
- **Cons**: This path delays aggressive revenue capture, and it requires playtest iteration because the current project notes require regression tests before changes and playtesting after each change ([local codebase brief](file:///Users/jamespelton/Apps/React/RobloxBot/CLAUDE.md)).
- **Effort estimate**: 2-3 focused weeks for onboarding UI, tutorial arrows, wave objective clarity, reward pacing, store surfacing, bug fixes, and analytics events; this estimate is based on the current project already having core systems such as classes, wave UI, shop, gems, loot, lobby, and DataStore persistence ([local codebase brief](file:///Users/jamespelton/Apps/React/RobloxBot/CLAUDE.md)).
- **Real-world evidence**: Roblox officially exposes retention, funnel events, acquisition, and monetization analytics, which implies creators are expected to diagnose onboarding, progression, and purchase conversion with measured funnels rather than subjective launch impressions ([Roblox funnel-events docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/funnel-events), [Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)).

#### Option 2: Balanced SKU stack with cosmetics, convenience, and light progression
- **Description**: Sell a small set of one-time passes, repeatable developer products, gem bundles, private/co-op convenience, and one optional subscription; Roblox defines passes as one-time Robux purchases for privileges, developer products as repeatable purchases such as currency or consumables, and subscriptions as recurring monthly benefits ([Roblox passes docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/passes.md), [Roblox developer-products docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products), [Roblox subscriptions docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/subscriptions)).
- **Pros**: A diversified SKU ladder lets different payer types spend at different levels while avoiding a hard paywall; Roblox supports pass analytics, developer-product analytics, and monetization KPIs such as conversion rate, paying users, ARPPU, and ARPDAU in Creator Dashboard ([Roblox passes docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/passes.md), [Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)).
- **Cons**: Monetization can damage trust if players perceive paid combat advantages as mandatory, and Roblox's own randomized virtual item policy warning on pass documentation means any paid random reward must be handled carefully and transparently ([Roblox passes docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/passes.md), [Roblox randomized virtual items policy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/virtual-items)).
- **Effort estimate**: 2-4 weeks for SKU definitions, MarketplaceService wiring, receipts, purchase reconciliation, in-game shop UX, analytics events, and balance tuning; this estimate is moderate because the project already has GemStoreManager, ClassShopManager, CosmeticsManager, LootSystem, CodeManager, and DataManager systems to integrate with ([local codebase brief](file:///Users/jamespelton/Apps/React/RobloxBot/CLAUDE.md)).
- **Real-world evidence**: Roblox officially recommends using MarketplaceService for in-experience pass purchases, `ProcessReceipt` for developer products, and analytics tabs to monitor item sales and net revenue ([Roblox passes docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/passes.md), [Roblox developer-products docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products), [Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)).

#### Option 3: Acquisition-led launch using short-form content, Roblox ads, and creator codes
- **Description**: Launch with a 30-day content calendar, TikTok/YouTube Shorts clips, Roblox Sponsored Experiences/Search Ads tests, Discord community events, and creator/influencer seeding; Roblox provides official Ads Manager and promotion documentation for creator advertising, and the Creator Dashboard acquisition view helps identify traffic sources such as discovery, search, and advertising ([Roblox Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager), [Roblox acquisition docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)).
- **Pros**: Paid and organic acquisition can create the player volume needed to test retention and monetization faster; Roblox's advertising docs describe Ads Manager as the interface for creating and managing advertising campaigns, and Roblox's acquisition analytics are designed to show where users come from ([Roblox Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager), [Roblox acquisition docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)).
- **Cons**: Buying traffic before retention and monetization are proven can burn budget; this is especially risky because the $500/month target requires repeatable gross Robux volume, not just visits, under the DevEx math above ([Roblox DevEx support](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/13061189551124-Developer-Exchange-Help-and-Information-Page), [Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)).
- **Effort estimate**: 1-2 weeks for launch assets, thumbnails, trailer clips, ad tests, analytics review, Discord setup, and creator-code redemption; this estimate is feasible because the game already has visual features such as boss cinematics, weather, loot roulette, upgraded lobby, enemy visuals, and promo-code infrastructure ([local codebase brief](file:///Users/jamespelton/Apps/React/RobloxBot/CLAUDE.md)).
- **Real-world evidence**: Roblox officially supports promotion through Ads Manager and sponsored experiences, while acquisition analytics provide source-level reporting for evaluating promotion performance ([Roblox promotion docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion), [Roblox Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager), [Roblox acquisition docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)).

#### Option 4: Creator Rewards and active-spender optimization as upside
- **Description**: Treat Creator Rewards as an upside revenue stream by increasing the number of qualifying active spenders and sessions over 10 minutes; Roblox's Creator Rewards documentation describes rewards for creators who drive engagement and audience growth, and third-party reporting of the 2025 program launch states a Daily Engagement Reward of 5 Robux for active spenders who spend at least 10 minutes in an experience ([Roblox Creator Rewards docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards), [The Verge coverage](http://www.theverge.com/news/691308/roblox-daily-engagement-audience-expansion-reward-program-payouts-creator-rewards)).
- **Pros**: Crystal Siege's wave-survival structure naturally supports 10-minute sessions because rounds, boss waves, shopping breaks, building upgrades, and endless mode already exist in the game design ([local codebase brief](file:///Users/jamespelton/Apps/React/RobloxBot/CLAUDE.md)).
- **Cons**: Creator Rewards should not be the base-case revenue model because eligibility, payout rules, and program details can change, and Roblox already replaced the older engagement-based payouts with newer creator rewards according to Roblox's own engagement-payout documentation and Creator Rewards documentation ([Roblox engagement-based payouts docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/engagement-based-payouts.md), [Roblox Creator Rewards docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards)).
- **Effort estimate**: 1-2 weeks after core retention polish for session-length rewards, comeback bonuses, active-spender funnels, and analytics events; this estimate assumes existing daily rewards, XP leveling, gems, and wave progression can be reused ([local codebase brief](file:///Users/jamespelton/Apps/React/RobloxBot/CLAUDE.md)).
- **Real-world evidence**: Roblox's Creator Rewards page is the authoritative program source, while media coverage confirms Roblox positioned the program as a way to reward creators for attracting and retaining players ([Roblox Creator Rewards docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards), [The Verge coverage](http://www.theverge.com/news/691308/roblox-daily-engagement-audience-expansion-reward-program-payouts-creator-rewards)).

### Monetization SKUs, Prices, and Math

#### Revenue target math
- **DevEx target**: $500/month ÷ $0.0038 per earned Robux = **131,579 earned Robux/month**, using Roblox's post-September-5-2025 rate of $0.0038/R$ ([Roblox DevEx support](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/13061189551124-Developer-Exchange-Help-and-Information-Page)).
- **Gross purchase target**: 131,579 earned Robux ÷ 70% estimated creator proceeds = **187,970 gross Robux/month**, using the commonly documented 70% creator share for developer products/pass sales ([Roblox developer-products docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)).
- **Base conversion model**: At **10% payer conversion** and **250 gross Robux monthly ARPPU**, Crystal Siege needs about **7,519 monthly active users**, because 7,519 users × 10% payers × 250 Robux × 70% × $0.0038 ≈ $500; Roblox's monetization analytics explicitly track conversion rate, paying users, ARPPU, and ARPDAU ([Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)).
- **Lower-conversion model**: At **5% payer conversion** and **250 gross Robux monthly ARPPU**, Crystal Siege needs about **15,038 monthly active users** to reach the same DevEx target; this scenario is useful for early launch planning because Roblox exposes both acquisition and monetization analytics for traffic-source and payer analysis ([Roblox acquisition docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition), [Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)).
- **Higher-ARPPU model**: At **8% payer conversion** and **400 gross Robux monthly ARPPU**, Crystal Siege needs about **5,874 monthly active users**, but this model requires stronger mid/high-tier SKUs and careful balance to avoid pay-to-win perception ([Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization), [Roblox randomized virtual items policy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/virtual-items)).

#### SKU implementation notes
- **Use developer products for gems and consumables** because Roblox defines developer products as repeatable purchases appropriate for in-game currency and consumable benefits ([Roblox developer-products docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)).
- **Use passes for durable identity and convenience** because Roblox defines passes as one-time purchases for special privileges inside an experience ([Roblox passes docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/passes.md)).
- **Use subscriptions only after retention is proven** because Roblox subscription design guidance emphasizes ongoing value, recurring benefits, and consistent delivery ([Roblox subscriptions docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/subscriptions), [Roblox subscription-design docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design)).
- **Avoid paid randomized rewards unless policy-compliant** because Roblox pass documentation explicitly warns creators to review the Randomized Virtual Item Policy before using passes as randomized rewards ([Roblox passes docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/passes.md), [Roblox randomized virtual items policy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/virtual-items)).
- **Personalize store ordering after initial sales** because Roblox documents `RankProductsAsync` and `RecommendTopProductsAsync`, with the caveat that top-product recommendations require at least one purchase in the past 28 days ([Roblox passes docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/passes.md)).

### Risks
1. **Pay-to-win backlash**: If paid classes, gems, or boosts become mandatory for survival, co-op trust may fall; mitigate with earnable paths, cosmetic/status SKUs, and modest convenience rather than permanent combat superiority ([Roblox subscription-design docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design), [Roblox randomized virtual items policy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/virtual-items)).
2. **Under-instrumented launch**: Without funnel and monetization events, it will be unclear whether failures come from onboarding, retention, acquisition source quality, store visibility, or pricing; Roblox provides funnel, acquisition, retention, and monetization analytics specifically for this diagnosis ([Roblox funnel-events docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/funnel-events), [Roblox acquisition docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition), [Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)).
3. **Ad spend waste**: Sponsored traffic can fail if early retention and shop conversion are weak; mitigate by gating ad spend behind Day 1 retention, 10-minute sessions, shop opens, and payer conversion thresholds ([Roblox Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager), [Roblox retention docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)).
4. **Policy and randomization risk**: Paid loot boxes or randomized paid rewards require compliance with Roblox's Randomized Virtual Item Policy, and the safer default is deterministic purchases plus transparent cosmetic rewards ([Roblox randomized virtual items policy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/virtual-items), [Roblox passes docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/passes.md)).
5. **Operational bandwidth**: Weekly live updates, analytics reviews, community management, and playtesting can exceed solo-dev capacity; mitigate by reusing existing systems such as weather, codes, bosses, cosmetics, loot, quests, and leaderboard features already present or listed in the project brief ([local codebase brief](file:///Users/jamespelton/Apps/React/RobloxBot/CLAUDE.md)).
6. **DevEx threshold and rate dependency**: The $500/month model depends on earned Robux and the current DevEx rate of $0.0038/R$ for Robux earned after September 5, 2025, so revenue plans should be tracked in earned Robux first and dollars second ([Roblox DevEx support](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/13061189551124-Developer-Exchange-Help-and-Information-Page)).
7. **Creator Rewards uncertainty**: Creator Rewards can improve upside but should not be counted as base-case revenue because program terms and eligibility are controlled by Roblox and have already evolved from previous engagement-based payout systems ([Roblox engagement-based payouts docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/engagement-based-payouts.md), [Roblox Creator Rewards docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards)).

### Recommendation
Choose **Option 1 + Option 2 as the core plan**, then layer **Option 3** only after retention and monetization baselines exist, while treating **Option 4** as upside rather than guaranteed revenue. The clearest $500/month path is to reach roughly **188k gross Robux/month** from a balanced SKU stack, which is achievable with either about **7.5k monthly users at 10% payer conversion and 250 Robux ARPPU** or about **5.9k monthly users at 8% payer conversion and 400 Robux ARPPU**, assuming 70% net earned Robux and $0.0038 DevEx conversion ([Roblox DevEx support](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/13061189551124-Developer-Exchange-Help-and-Information-Page), [Roblox developer-products docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products), [Roblox monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)). The strongest implementation sequence is: polish first session and reward clarity, launch deterministic SKUs, instrument every funnel, run a controlled soft launch, then buy or push traffic only after analytics prove the game can retain and convert players ([Roblox funnel-events docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/funnel-events), [Roblox acquisition docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition), [Roblox retention docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)).
