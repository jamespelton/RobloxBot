# Sponsored Experience — Ad Copy Variants

For Roblox Creator Hub → Ads → Sponsored Experiences.

**3 ad copy variants** to test in rotation. Watch CTR (clicks/impressions) per variant for 48 hours each, then double down on the winner.

---

## Variant 1 — "POV / Discovery" angle

**Headline:** Defend the Crystal!
**Tagline:** 6 classes · 30+ buildings · co-op bosses

**Why it works:**
- Action-verb headline ("Defend!") drives kid attention
- Tagline crams 3 value props in 6 words
- Genre-clear, no false promise

---

## Variant 2 — "Number-driven" angle

**Headline:** 6 Classes. Infinite Waves.
**Tagline:** Can you survive Wave 50?

**Why it works:**
- Numbers grab attention in scroll
- "Can you" hooks kids who love challenges
- "Survive" + "Wave 50" implies depth without overclaiming

---

## Variant 3 — "Free reward" angle

**Headline:** Free Gems Inside! 💎
**Tagline:** Code: CRYSTALSIEGE — claim 100 gems

**Why it works:**
- "Free" + emoji grab attention
- Concrete number (100 gems) feels achievable
- The CODE callout converts curious clickers → engaged players (they ENTER the game to claim, which counts as a play)

⚠️ **Compliance check:** Roblox sometimes flags "Free" in ads as misleading if the game has paid items. Test for 24h and watch for ad rejection notices.

---

## Targeting (apply to all 3 variants)

- **Age:** 9–13 (primary), 14–17 (secondary)
- **Geography:** US, UK, CA (English-speaking, highest ARPDAU)
- **Device:** Mobile-first (75% budget), Desktop (25%)
- **Genre filter:** "Tower Defense", "Survival", "RPG"
- **Time:** After-school (3-6pm local) + weekends

| Days 3-4 | $5/day on Variant 2 | Compare against V1 |

**Hard cap for week 1:** $35. **Hard cap for weeks 1-12:** $1,500 (per roadmap).

---

## What to Watch

- **CTR (click-through rate)** — Industry benchmark for Roblox Sponsored Experiences is ~1-3%. Below 0.8% = pull the creative.
- **CPC (cost per click)** — Benchmark ~$0.25. Above $0.40 = something's off (probably icon or targeting).
- **Play-through rate** (visits / clicks) — Should be 60-80%. Below 50% = your icon won the click but the game page is killing them (description or screenshots wrong).
- **D1 retention** of users acquired via ads — Compare against organic D1. If ad-acquired D1 is <50% of organic, you're buying the wrong audience.

---

## Kill Criteria

Stop ad spend immediately if:
- CTR is <0.5% across all 3 variants after 48 hours
- D1 retention is <15% on ad-acquired users
- Like ratio drops below 70% (ad traffic souring the community)
- You hit $1,500 cumulative without sustained 200+ CCU

In all cases, the next step is **fix retention before spending more**, not new ads.
