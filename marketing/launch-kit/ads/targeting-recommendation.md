# Sponsored Experience — Targeting Recommendation

## Audience

### Primary (80% of budget)

- **Age:** 9–13
- **Geography:** United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia
- **Language:** English
- **Device:** Mobile (iOS + Android) — 75%, Desktop — 25%

**Why this audience:**
- 9-13 is the peak Roblox demographic with the highest spend rate (parental Robux cards)
- US/UK/CA/AU are the highest-ARPDAU regions
- Mobile is where 70%+ of Roblox playtime happens for this age group

### Secondary (20% of budget — optional, for week 5+)

- **Age:** 14–17 (older Roblox players, higher retention but lower spend)
- **Geography:** Same English-speaking regions
- **Device:** Mobile-first

**Defer this until week 5+** — focus on the primary audience first.

---

## Interest Targeting

Roblox Sponsored Experiences let you target by genre affinity. Layer these on top of the demographic:

- **Tower Defense Simulator** (direct competitor — high-intent users)
- **Anime Defenders** (similar genre)
- **Tower Battles** (older TD game, dedicated fans)
- **All Star Tower Defense** (anime-themed TD)
- **Tower Defenders** (newer TD entrant)

⚠️ **CAUTION:** Some of these are big established games. Targeting their fans will get clicks but those users may be picky. Watch retention closely — these users churn fast if your FTUE doesn't impress in the first 60 seconds.

---

## Negative Targeting (exclude)

- **Roleplay-only audiences** — wrong genre fit
- **Obby-only audiences** — wrong genre fit
- **Simulator-game fans without TD overlap** — different player psychology
- **Russia, China, India** for week 1 — high impression cost relative to ARPDAU. Add back in week 6+ if ad spend is converting.

---

## Time-of-Day

Schedule ads to run during peak Roblox hours:

| Time (Local) | Day Type | Why |
|--------------|----------|-----|
| 3:00 PM – 7:00 PM | Weekdays | After-school window. Highest concurrent users. |
| 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM | Saturdays | Peak weekend window. |
| 12:00 PM – 6:00 PM | Sundays | Lower spend day (per James's Sunday policy — but ads keep running automatically). |
| 11:00 PM – 6:00 AM | Any day | PAUSE — low conversion, kids asleep. |

If Roblox Ads doesn't let you schedule by hour, set the daily cap so it spends out by 9pm and doesn't run overnight.

## Performance Benchmarks (Roblox Sponsored Experiences, late 2025 data)

| Metric | Below Average | Average | Strong | Excellent |
|--------|--------------:|--------:|-------:|----------:|
| **CTR** | <0.8% | 1-3% | 3-5% | >5% |
| **CPC** | >$0.40 | $0.20-0.30 | $0.10-0.20 | <$0.10 |
| **Visit-to-Play** | <50% | 60-75% | 75-85% | >85% |
| **D1 (ad-acquired)** | <15% | 18-25% | 25-35% | >35% |

Track these in a simple spreadsheet daily. If you're below "Average" for 3 days running on any metric, pause and adjust before more spend.

---

## When NOT to Run Sponsored Experiences

- **D1 retention is below 20% on organic users.** Fix retention first. Ads on a leaky bucket just speed up the leak.
- **Like ratio is below 70%.** Negative reviews drag CTR down across the platform.
- **You haven't iterated the icon yet.** Always test icon before scaling ad spend — bad icon = wasted impressions.
- **You don't have time to monitor.** Ads need daily check-ins for the first 7 days. If you can't watch the dashboard, don't run the ads.
- **Sunday/Sabbath (per James's policy).** Ads keep running automatically — just don't actively manage them on Sunday.
