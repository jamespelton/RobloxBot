DO'S AND DON'TS:

✅ DO personalize by mentioning a specific video they made
✅ DO use first names — feels human, not corporate
✅ DO put the offer up-front (500 Robux + tag + code)
✅ DO sign off with your real name + role + email
✅ DO mention Hayden — the dad-and-son angle is genuinely interesting
✅ DO follow up ONCE after 7 days, then move on

❌ DON'T mass-email 30 people from the same thread. Send one at a time.
❌ DON'T promise revenue share or affiliate income — too complicated, breaks at scale
❌ DON'T promise more than you can deliver. Under-promise, over-deliver.
❌ DON'T be pushy. One follow-up max. No third email.
❌ DON'T claim you'll go viral with their help. Be honest about being small.
❌ DON'T pretend to be a bigger company than you are.

---

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS:

- Reply rate: 5-15% (the doc said this, real-world Roblox creator outreach is closer to 5%)
- "Yes to cover" rate of those who reply: 20-40%
- So 30 emails → 1.5-4 actual videos

For 30 sends:
- 2-5 replies (positive or negative)
- 1-2 videos within 30 days

If you get ZERO replies after 30 sends, the email is broken — not the audience. Iterate on the subject line first, then the offer.
