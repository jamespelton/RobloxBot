# Crystal Siege — 30-Day TikTok Content Calendar

**Goal:** 1 short per day for 30 days. Pre-record a batch in 1-2 weekend sessions to avoid daily treadmill.

**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 15-30 seconds, hook in the FIRST 1.5 SECONDS, end-card with "Play Crystal Siege on Roblox" + game thumbnail.

**Why this works:** TikTok and YouTube Shorts are now the dominant Roblox discovery channels for 9-13. Algorithm rewards completion rate + comments. Hooks must commit IMMEDIATELY.

---

## Recording Plan (do this once, batch-style)

**Session 1 (1-2 hours):** Capture raw gameplay for these scenarios. Record longer than you need — you'll cut in editing:
- Boss kill on Wave 10 (Dark Commander) — 30s clip
- Boss kill on Wave 5 (Berserker) — 30s clip
- Wave 49 → 50 clutch save in endless mode — 60s clip
- Full base build from empty to maxed-out — 5min raw, 10x speedup in edit

- Each of the 6 classes using their ability once — 6 × 10s clips
- A "wipe" — entire team dies — 15s clip
- Each weather effect (sunny/rainy/snowy) — 10s each

**Session 2 (1 hour):** Capture face-cam reactions for hooks that need you on-camera. Hayden can do these if he's available.

**Session 3 (2-3 hours):** Edit and schedule. Use CapCut on phone or Davinci on desktop. Add captions (TikTok auto-captions are okay, manual is better).

## Trending Audio Strategy

**TikTok algorithm prioritizes videos using trending sounds.** Check the TikTok Creative Center → Sounds → Trending the morning you post.

For Roblox kid audiences specifically, the sounds that work best are:
- "POV" voiceover formats
- Tier-list narration sounds
- "Watch this..." dramatic buildup tracks
- Boss/monster intro music (for boss fight content)
- "Did you know" pro-tip sounds

**RULE:** If a sound has fewer than 1k uses, skip it (not trending yet). If it has more than 100k, skip it (saturated). Sweet spot: 5k-50k uses.

## Hashtag Strategy

Use 4-6 hashtags per post. Pattern:
- 1 broad: `#roblox` or `#fyp`
- 2 medium: `#robloxgame`, `#towerdefense`, `#robloxcode`
- 1-2 niche: `#crystalsiege`, `#robloxtd`
- 1 trend-tied: whatever's trending that day

Don't stuff 15 hashtags. TikTok doesn't reward that anymore.
