## What you need to record yourself

A 12-15 second clip of yourself saying the lines above, looking enthusiastic. You can do it in ONE take — just talk through it naturally. If you mess up, restart. Total recording time: 5 minutes including a couple retakes.

## What you DON'T need

- Gameplay footage (the wordmark graphic + the cover image carry the visuals)
- A microphone (phone audio is fine for selfie-cam content)
- Editing skills beyond drop-in clips

## Editing flow in TikTok or CapCut (5 minutes)

1. Import your selfie clip
2. Cut to 0:07 — drop in `wordmark-overlay.png` as image overlay for 3s
3. Resume selfie at 0:11
4. At 0:14, cut to `day1-cover.png` as static end card
5. Add caption text overlays at the times shown above (TikTok built-in text tool is fine)
6. Add trending audio over the whole thing (DUCK your voice under the audio at 50%)
7. Trim and tighten — should land at 14-15 sec total

## Why this teaser works without gameplay

- **The dad-and-12yo-son angle is the genuinely interesting hook** for kid audiences (their parents, their siblings) and Roblox creator-discovery algorithms
- **Trending audio + selfie cam** = native to the platform, doesn't feel like a corporate ad
- **The wordmark + cover image carry the visual brand** so it doesn't look low-effort
- **CTA is verbal (Search Crystal Siege)** because TikTok kids don't click bio links
