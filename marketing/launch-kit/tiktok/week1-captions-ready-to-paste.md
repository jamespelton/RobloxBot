## Important conventions

- **Link in bio:** Roblox game URL → `https://roblox.com/games/70438125046613/Crystal-Siege`
- **End every video** with text overlay: "Search Crystal Siege on Roblox" (kid audience doesn't click links)
- **Pin Day 1 video** to top of profile after posting (long-press → Pin)
- **Reply to EVERY comment** in the first 24h after posting — biggest algo boost lever
- **Don't delete flops** — just learn from them. Deletes hurt account standing.
