**Title:** Feedback on Crystal Siege, a co-op wave defense game

Hey everyone,

I've been building a co-op wave defense game called Crystal Siege [CONFIRM: with my son Hayden], and I'd love honest feedback from people who know what makes these games work.

**Game:** https://www.roblox.com/games/70438125046613/Crystal-Siege

**What it is**

You and your team protect a crystal in the middle of an arena from 10 waves of enemies, then keep going in endless mode. Everyone picks one of six classes (Fighter, Healer, Builder, Mage, Shooter, Recruiter). Between waves you spend coins on defenses: walls, spike traps, turrets, cannons, healing stations and a few others, each with three upgrade tiers. There's a boss at wave 5 and wave 10.

**What I'd like feedback on**

1. **First five minutes.** Is it clear what to do after you spawn?
2. **Classes.** Does any one feel useless or way too strong?
3. **Building.** Is placing and upgrading defenses satisfying, or is it a chore?
4. **Difficulty.** Where did you lose, and did it feel fair?
5. **Solo play.** Is it fun alone, or does it need a group?

I'll read every reply and I'm happy to play your games and leave feedback too.

Thanks for taking a look!

James
