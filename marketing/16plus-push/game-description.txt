Defend the Crystal from waves of enemies with your friends!

🎯 6 classes: Fighter, Healer, Builder, Mage, Shooter, Recruiter
🏰 9 kinds of defenses (walls, spike traps, turrets, cannons, healing stations and more), each with 3 upgrade tiers
👹 Boss fights at wave 5 and wave 10, then endless mode
⚔️ Scouts, Tanks, Archers and Bombers, all coming for the Crystal
🛟 Get knocked down? A teammate can revive you
🎁 Loot chests, daily rewards and gems
🌧️ Weather that changes: sunny, rain, snow

HOW TO PLAY
1. Pick a class
2. Build and upgrade defenses between waves
3. Keep the Crystal alive
4. See how far you get in endless mode

💎 Use code CRYSTALSIEGE for free gems
